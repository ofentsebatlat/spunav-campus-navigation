About the Institution

Sol Plaatje University (SPU) is a public university located in Kimberley, South Africa, established to provide high-quality education in a modern urban setting.

Our campus is a hub of innovation, blending historical significance with state-of-the-art facilities. We are committed to academic excellence and community engagement, fostering an environment where students can thrive.
